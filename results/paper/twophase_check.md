# Reference-rate strata for a claim about the prompt source

`scripts/twophase_check.py`. Every replication draws a pool of N prompts from the stored pool (the source), rebuilds the 8 strata on it, and draws the safety set from that pool; the truth is the source's rate. 10,000 replications a cell; cells and labels as in `replacement_check.md`. Classes by the paper's rule.
