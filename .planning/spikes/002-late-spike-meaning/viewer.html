TD-error spikes in the synthetic Seldonian runs

Each run trains 300 steps. The dashed line at step 200 is where a run would stop; steps after it show what happens if training continues. The TD error is the agent's own (online critic): valence is its mean per step (positive = better than expected). Red ticks mark the spikes, jumps in mean |TD error| more than 2 robust sd, counted over the first 200 steps.
