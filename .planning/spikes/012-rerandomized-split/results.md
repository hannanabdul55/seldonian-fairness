## C. Superseded first settings (kept for the t-test finding)

n = 1000, tau = 0.05, 41-point grid, project t-test bound (`runs_old/`): 35% of returned candidates were the trivial 'predict all positive' pair (thresholds -3, -3), whose safety-set bound has zero width (TPR = 1 in both groups); 'uncovered' was 0.35 in every arm, and 0.0005 once those are excluded. With Clopper-Pearson, a 64-seed pilot over tau in {0.1, 0.15, 0.2, 0.3} x n in {1000, 3000} x inflation {1, 2} found no true violation in any cell: the four-tail union bound is too conservative to show a leak, which is why section A uses the tight Wald test.
