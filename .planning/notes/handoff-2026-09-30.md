## Where work stopped: idea `prompted-ghat` (spikes 015-018)

`g_hat` from natural language. A four-researcher pass found the space largely unpublished:
compiling text into a *statistical* constraint, and certifying through a corrected proxy
label, both appear to be gaps. Jev (TypeSafe, released 2026-09-15) is a typed-decision API
whose Noul question is exactly this measurement, but it is proprietary and unmeasured on
safety judging, and on 2026-09-30 the user ruled out using it (no access, and no sending data
off the machine).

**015 is done (PARTIAL).** Full write-up in
`.planning/spikes/015-prompted-judge-fidelity/README.md`. The three results that constrain
everything after it:
- a **verifiable** constraint (brevity, "more than 80 words") must compile to *code*, not a
  judge: the compiled judge is at chance (AUC 0.500) and its aggregate rate matched the truth
  by coincidence on noise labels;
- paraphrases preserve the **ranking** (harm AUC 0.80-0.87) and destroy the **operating
  point** (measured rate 0.09-0.46), so a certificate must pin the exact wording and a
  calibrated threshold;
- the compiled judge **matches Qwen3Guard-4B** on harm (FA 0.216 vs 0.230, caught 2/3 vs
  1/3), and both are unusable raw at a 1.3% base rate.
- The **rubric-artifact test fired on refusal** (blank AUC 0.694), so spike 018's gate is open.

## Spike 016 (prompt-to-spec-compile): done the same day, PARTIAL

Full write-up in `.planning/spikes/016-prompt-to-spec-compile/README.md`; one table for all
arms in `summary.md`; `viewer.html` shows every sentence and what each arm compiled;
`./try.sh` compiles whatever you type.
- The **deterministic half is exact**: a DSL line or JSON spec builds the project's own
  constraint classes and reproduces the hand-written g to the last bit (8 constraints x 5
  data sets). The model never picks `monotone`, the delta split or the bound.
- The **pre-registered prompt failed** (3 of 8 sentences; a silent error on 20 of 39
  faithful wordings). A redesigned one (flat prompt groups, the reference only through
  `REF`, one example per construct) with thinking gives 7 of 8 certificates, 8 of 8
  requirements, 34 of 39 wordings and no silent error.
- It still needs **guards**: a lint that the reference model satisfies a relative
  constraint, a lint that every number in the spec occurs in the sentence, and a second
  independent compile. With them: 46 right and 1 wrong of 47 accepted, 18 of 64 clear
  sentences sent back as a question.
- **"Relative to the reference" is three certificates** (constant, two-sample, paired:
  widths 0.029 / 0.077 / 0.039 at 500 prompts). Round 6's `reference + margin` is the
  constant one and ignores the reference's sampling error. The builder should own this
  choice; this is a finding about Rounds 1-6 as much as about the compiler.

## Spike 017 (calibration-carrying-certificate): done the same day, PARTIAL

Full write-up in `.planning/spikes/017-calibration-carrying-certificate/README.md`; tables in
`results.md`; the three certificates in `cards.md`; `viewer.html` lets you draw labels and
watch each route's bound. **Not committed**: it finished inside the weekday 9-5 PT window.
- The certificate is **gold labels on the responses being certified, with the judge as a
  variance reducer**, routed by a fixed rule: code for verifiable properties; Clopper-Pearson
  on the labels below 10 in the rarer class; PPI++ on the judge's logit with a bootstrap-t
  limit otherwise; design weights and `b1w` for the stratified sheet. Largest miss 0.053.
- **PPI++ with its normal limit is not usable** (0.08-0.13 at 100-225 labels, 0.20-0.24 at a
  1.3% rate), and no finite-sample judge-assisted bound beat the labels alone.
- **The logit, not p**: rho^2 0.48 against 0.22, worth 1.6x the labels against 1.16x.
- **A calibration does not carry across prompt populations** (recall differs in 4 of 6
  wordings; a carried bound missed in up to 94% of draws). It held across 200 training steps.
- **Harm:** the sheet certifies 0.039 for the 4,800 screened responses by its weighted labels;
  the compiled judge adds nothing (rho^2 0.02). Read as i.i.d., the sheet certified a negative
  harm rate under one wording. Qwen3Guard-4B's false-alarm rate is 0.120 weighted, not 0.230.
- One judge-only GPU pass was added (35 min): `results/spikes/017/scores_pop.jsonl`.
